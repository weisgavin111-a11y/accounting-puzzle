1Classify the lease

A lessee has a finance lease if any one of the five criteria in ASC 842-10-25-2 is met. Otherwise it's an operating lease.

    2Measure at commencement

    The liability is the present value of payments not yet made. The right-of-use asset starts from the liability, then adds payments made at or before commencement and initial direct costs, and subtracts incentives received.
